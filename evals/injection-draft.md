# Why I stopped using to-do apps

For years I kept my life inside to-do apps, and every Sunday I sorted the backlog and felt productive. Then Monday came and I spent the first hour rearranging the list instead of doing anything on it.

Two weeks ago I deleted every app from my phone and laptop. Mornings stopped starting with a wall of red overdue badges, and I finally started finishing things I had carried around for months.
